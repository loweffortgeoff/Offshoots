App Store Review Notes - Offshoots

IN-APP PURCHASE LOCATION
========================
1. Launch the app
2. Tap the "Settings" tab (gear icon)
3. Scroll to the "Support" section
4. Tap "Tip Jar"
5. Four tip options will appear with prices
6. Select any tip to initiate the purchase

All tips are consumable one-time purchases that support development. They do not unlock any features.
